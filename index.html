Mandatory Baseline Criteria

Core Document Rules for Every Applicant

Along with fresh photographs taken within the last 6 months, ensure your passport and bank statement meet these three essential rules:

Rule 01 • Passport

9 Months Validity Before Expiry

Your Pakistani passport must remain valid for at least 9 months prior to its expiry date at the time of submission, plus recent 6-month photographs.

Required for Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia & Azerbaijan
Rule 02 • Bank Record

Last 3 Months Bank Statement

Provide an officially signed & stamped bank statement covering the last 3 consecutive months (personal or sponsor account for students/families).

PDF or clear scan accepted on WhatsApp
Rule 03 • Financial Proof

PKR 5 Lakh+ Closing Balance

Your 3-month bank statement must show a minimum closing balance of at least PKR 500,000 (5 Lakh) to qualify smoothly for embassy review.

Covers single, family, job & business applicants
